How long does a website take?

Most sites are ready within 1 to 2 weeks of me having your text and photos. I'll give you a clear timeline when we agree the project.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo (if you have one), a few photos, and a short description of your business and services. If you don't have these yet, I'll help you work out what's needed.

How does payment work?

50% upfront to start the project and 50% when you're happy and the site is ready to launch.

Yes. Once the site is paid for, it's yours. If you ever want to move hosting or work with someone else, I'll hand everything over.

Small changes are covered by your monthly plan. For bigger changes like new pages, I'll give you a fixed price before I start.

Yes. I can rebuild an outdated site, keep the content that still works, and move your existing domain across.
